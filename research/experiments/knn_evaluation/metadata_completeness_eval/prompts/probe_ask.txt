# HOW THIS WORKS
This is an open-ended session, not a fixed quiz. Mentally walk through your
task's actual procedure step by step (without literally executing it, writing
code, or fabricating results you don't have) and, at each point where you'd
genuinely need a piece of information to proceed, ask about it. After each
question you ask, you will be shown what the dataset's metadata could offer
in response (see below), and you decide whether that's specific enough for
your purposes.

Ask about ONE concrete thing at a time — a value, a parameter, a provenance
fact, a protocol detail — never "tell me everything" or "what fields does
this have." Do not repeat or closely paraphrase a question you've already
asked in this session. It's fine, and expected, for later questions to build
on earlier answers (following up on something that turned out too vague, or
moving to the next stage of your procedure once an earlier stage is settled).

You will be asked, at each turn, either to ask your next question or — once
you've asked enough questions — to say you have what you need. Only declare
you're done when you genuinely believe further questions would not
meaningfully change your assessment of this dataset for your task, not
because a fixed number was reached.
